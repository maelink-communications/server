## Available clients for this server

### Pancake
By: NotFenixio<br>
What you can do: Sign in, register, see Home posts, edit your bio. That's about it.<br>
Where: <https://notfenixio.is-a.dev/pancake/><br>
GitHub: <https://github.com/NotFenixio/pancake><br>
**Verified to work on**: v0.3.3b
### yorlink
By: zagje<br>
What you can do: Basically anything that isn't related to Bubbles or user editing. So, sign in, register, see Home and actually post and interact with Home posts!<br>
Where: <https://zag.lunarsphere.net/yorlink/><br>
GitHub: <https://github.com/Zagyen8913/yorlink><br>
**Verified to work on**: v0.3.3b
### waechain
By: Kabez<br>
What you can do: Login, register (as of a2), see Home posts (as logged in AND guest as of a2), make posts, log out.<br>
Where: <https://kabezz.github.io/waechain-maelink/><br>
GitHub: <https://github.com/Kabezz/waechain-maelink><br>
**Verified to work on**: v0.4.0
### Solstice
By: Milo the Protogen<br>
What you can do: Login, register, see Home posts, make posts, like posts, delete posts, edit posts, view inbox.<br>
Where: <https://solstice52.github.io/><br>
GitHub: <https://github.com/solstice52/solstice52.github.io/><br>
**Verified to work on**: v0.4.1c
